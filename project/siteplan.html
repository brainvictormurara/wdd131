AgroVision Zimbabwe

Website Planning Document

Site Name

AgroVision Zimbabwe

This name represents a modern agriculture-focused website designed to educate and support farmers in Zimbabwe.

Optional Domain: agrovisionzim.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide agricultural information, farming tips, crop management guides, and sustainable farming techniques.

  • Crop production information
  • Modern farming methods
  • Pest and disease control
  • Irrigation guidance

Scenarios

  • What crops grow best during Zimbabwe’s rainy season?
  • How can I control pests naturally without damaging crops?
  • What are the best soil preparation methods before planting?
